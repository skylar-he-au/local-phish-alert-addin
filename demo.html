Demo inbox and email check

Emails are read on this computer and checked by the local AI. Nothing is uploaded.

    Open the demo folder (demo/emails in the project), choose saved .eml files, or drop them here. Links and images in the emails are removed before they are shown.

    Demo samples: Phishing Pot (CC BY-NC 4.0), Apache SpamAssassin public corpus, Enron corpus, and the team's synthetic BEC set.
